# Rule: Presales Response

## Scope and authority

Apply these criteria to presales answers and their internal evidence records. They supplement [documentation management](./workflow-documentation.md), [Chinese technical writing](./writing-chinese-technical.md) when applicable, and the adopting project's disclosure and service policies. They define no product capability, price, deployment default or service commitment.

## Criteria

| Concern | Pass condition and evidence to inspect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Original request | Original questions and identifiers remain intact; each requested scope, number, executor, charge and attachment maps to an answer or an explicit unresolved fact. Compare with the original, including subquestions. |
| Claim status | Internal records distinguish product fact, user confirmation, delivery proposal, reference estimate and proposed commitment. Inspect source locators, version/environment, confirmation provenance and approval status. A confirmation remains attributed unless independently verified; scope labels do not imply verification. |
| Deployment proof | Static implementation supports only the inspected mechanism. Claims of enabled configuration, end-to-end operation, certification or measured outcome have evidence for the stated environment and scope. |
| Metrics | Every number has its metric, unit, period, population, basis and applicable conditions. Historical elapsed time comes from event records, never an SLA. Release counts come from formal records and a consistent classification, never iteration cadence or commit counts. Distinguish product patches from vulnerability intelligence and affected versions from component names. |
| Estimates | An evidence-backed reference range is allowed without customer measurement when explicitly labelled as estimated and scoped to the metric and conditions. Network RTT is not application response time; do not collapse regions, nodes, modules or network conditions into one unsupported number. |
| Service boundaries | Execution, support and fees are separately supported by the applicable agreement or authorized proposal. Distinguish product entitlement, packages, instructions, remote support, on-site work and special services. Deployment names or maintenance coverage alone prove none of these. Distinguish response, mitigation and final repair. |
| Answer language | The answer directly supplies the requested result in a professional provider voice with clear conditions. A process description or generic recommendation does not replace a fact. Personnel or strategy recommendations are appropriate when requested, with their rationale. Positive wording never promotes a proposal into an existing capability. |
| Disclosure | Customer material presents results and applicable conditions; internal investigation, tasks, unapproved plans, personnel details and other customers' information remain outside it. Inspect the final body and attachments. Relevant customer-specific facts remain in that customer's layer. |
| Delivery claims | Every statement that material is provided has a reviewable attachment or an explicit agreed delivery scope and status. Future delivery is labelled and authorized; do not silently postpone a requested attachment until contracting. Inspect files and references. |
| Reuse and freshness | Reusable answers record topic, originating customer/tenant separately from applicability, long-term or temporary status, verification date, evidence and review trigger. Mechanisms are rechecked on relevant version changes; releases, prices, security intelligence, latency and service terms are rechecked for the time and project conditions before reuse. |

## Remediation

Correct the owning record and regenerate affected answers or attachments. For a critical gap, seek the missing evidence or authorized decision; retain a visible gap when unavailable. Narrow a claim to its supported scope rather than inventing a fact, deleting the customer's question or substituting a workflow. Use this table as the single presales quality checklist.
