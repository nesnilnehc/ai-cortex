# Working layouts

Use only when the adopting project's records do not already carry the needed information. Expand complex answers into separate claims instead of assigning one evidence status to an entire paragraph. The [presales Rule](../../../rules/writing-presales-response.md) owns the criteria; these are editable working aids.

## Internal question and evidence map

| Original ID and verbatim question | Requested results | Proposed answer/claim | Claim status | Evidence locator and relevant passage | Version/environment and conditions | Gap or conflict and owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Record metric definitions, measurement window and calculation basis next to numerical claims. Record attributed confirmations with who confirmed what and when. Keep commitment approval status separate from capability evidence.

## Reusable knowledge entry

| Topic and canonical answer link | Originating customer/tenant | Applicability | Long-term/temporary | Verified on | Evidence link and status | Review trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Origin may be restricted metadata. Customer origin does not imply customer-only applicability. An index carries navigation and scope, while the FAQ owns the standard answer.

## Customer result and attachment map

| Original ID and verbatim question | Direct answer and applicable conditions | Attachment reference or agreed delivery scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

Do not export the internal evidence map as a customer worksheet. Keep filenames, question identifiers and attachment references consistent with the actual files; capture final approval state in the customer record.
