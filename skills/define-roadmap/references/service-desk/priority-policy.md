# Service Desk requirement priority policy

Fictional reusable policy, authorized by the product owner at W0. It governs
relative treatment precedence; placement remains a source-backed planning decision.

| Priority | Project meaning |
| --- | --- |
| P0 | Active-customer data-integrity blockers |
| P1 | Capability improvements with a confirmed user |
| P2 | Unvalidated convenience requests |
| P3 | Explicit long-term ideas |

Preserve settled priorities unless a policy application or authorized reassessment
changes them. When a real choice between same-level items is needed, use the
confirmed delivery conditions and owner decision; an ID-based display order is not
business precedence. Readiness checks concern the next step's required deliverable,
not completion of an entire supplying requirement. Do not lower a priority merely
to explain a deferral or automatically raise a supplier's whole scope.

Current values live in [source records](sources.md#work-items). The
[W0 policy application](priority-decisions.md) and [placement decision](placement-decisions.md)
are retained decision snapshots, not another editable current inventory. Read the
[example navigation](README.md) for the dated planning and delivery observations.
